Supply Chain · Analytics · Web App

Uphopro — Digital Supply Chain & Inventory Optimization Platform

Personal Project · Wix Velo · 2024

Context

Upholstered manufacturing firms juggling heterogeneous leather hide defect clusters, polyurethane foam vs. imported cover lead-time gaps, and desynchronized cut-and-sew lines.

Problem

High floor congestion, excessive cutting scrap, decoupled procurement cycles, and assembly line takt time misalignment driving production inefficiency.

Approach

Formulated dynamic Reorder Points (ROP) with supplier lead-time variance modelling, 2D nesting yield optimization algorithms, and ABC-XYZ inventory matrix segmentation.

Solution

Designed a Wix Velo web application integrating raw material inventory tracking, BOM management, automated quote generation, and real-time kitting dashboards.

Impact

Reduced floor congestion, synchronized assembly kitting, and brought measurable operational gains to the production cycle.

✅ Cutting Scrap < 3% ✅ Kitting Synchronized ✅ Floor Congestion Reduced
Product Engineering · Quality · DFMEA

Footwear Product Engineering & DFMEA Implementation

Tata / Calsea — Puma & Comet Accounts · Graduate Engineer Trainee · 2023–2025

Context

Graduate Engineer Trainee & Technical Marketing Liaison managing high-profile footwear brands Puma and Comet from technical design through mass production.

Problem

Pattern mismatches, sole delamination, and seam bursts were migrating from technical design phase into mass production, causing critical rejections and rework.

Approach

Built cross-functional DFMEA workflows connecting 2D/3D CAD design freeze, SATRA peel adhesion validation, and 3-gate pilot trial sign-off protocols.

Solution

Standardized SOPs across lasting and stitching lines, embedded quality gates at each production stage, and created a technical liaison bridge between design and manufacturing teams.

Impact

Eliminated critical bonding rejections, standardized cross-line production quality, and streamlined cut-to-box timelines for two international footwear brands.

✅ Zero Critical Rejections ✅ SOPs Standardized ✅ Cut-to-Box Streamlined
