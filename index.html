About

From the factory floor to the boardroom.

I started on production lines, reading hide defects, sole bonds and seam stress the way others read spreadsheets. Now I am taking that systems-level understanding into marketing and sustainable supply chains, because the best story about a product starts with knowing exactly how it is made.

The maker’s eye

At Tata International I managed footwear for Puma and Comet from technical design to mass production: pattern mismatches, sole detachment and seam bursts, caught before they reached the line, through DFMEA and gated trials.

The systems view

I standardised SOPs across lasting and stitching, tied CAD design freeze to adhesion validation, and built Uphopro to model inventory, cutting yield and supplier lead times for upholstery manufacturers.

The next chapter

Sustainable materials, honest product storytelling and data-driven supply chains. The MBA is where I join engineering rigour to brand and business strategy.
